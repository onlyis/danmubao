隐私政策

本隐私政策适用于闪念卡片 iOS 应用及关联学习服务。我们区分游客本地数据与登录用户云端数据。

1. 服务与联系

有关访问、更正、删除个人信息及隐私疑问，请联系 john@smartnote.ai。处理敏感请求前，我们可能需要核验账号归属。

2. 收集和处理的数据

账号与认证：注册登录所用的账号标识（如手机号）、昵称、受保护的登录凭据及必要的安全记录。学习数据：你创建的卡组、卡片、答案、收藏、复习进度、学习日志、偏好及分享设置。客服数据：你主动提交的邮件、截图和故障描述。

3. 数据用途

用于账号登录、安全验证、显示学习内容、计算复习进度、存储卡组、云端同步、异常排查和响应支持请求，不以出售你的学习内容或跨应用个性化广告为目的。

4. 本地与云端存储

游客卡片和学习记录可以保存在当前设备。登录后，为实现账号关联和多端同步，部分内容、设置和必要的同步状态会经网络传输到服务端。离线、同步失败或切换账户可能影响数据可用性。删除设备上的 App 不等于自动删除云端数据。

5. 系统权限及分享

仅在你主动保存图片或分享卡片等操作时使用 iOS 的图片保存、文件选择和分享功能。分享至其他应用后由接收方独立处理。普通卡片阅读不需要通讯录或精确定位权限。

6. 服务提供方与安全措施

基础网络、托管和设备系统服务可能处理完成请求所必需的技术信息。我们采取传输保护、访问限制和权限管理等合理措施，但任何系统均无法保证绝对安全。

7. 保存与删除

本地内容取决于设备保存状态，登录数据在账户及相关内容存续期间保存。必要的安全运维记录会在合理期限内保存，法律规定时可能延长。你可在 App 中删除卡片和卡组；账号及云端关联数据删除可通过「账号与安全」或 john@smartnote.ai 申请并完成身份核验。删除前请导出需要保留的材料。

8. 未成年人

未成年人应在监护人指导下使用。若发现不适合处理的未成年人信息，请联系支持邮箱申请核查。

9. 个人信息权利与跨境访问

你可以请求访问、更正、删除数据，并通过 iOS 系统设置管理权限。跨地区访问时信息可能由不同地区的基础设施处理，我们将按照适用法律履行义务。

10. 变更及生效

我们会随功能变化更新政策，重要变动会采取合理方式提示。生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日。

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Flash Thoughts Cards iOS app and related services. Guest data on your device is different from account-linked cloud data.

1. Service and contact

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, write to john@smartnote.ai. We may verify account ownership before processing sensitive requests.

2. Information we process

Account: an identifier such as your phone number, nickname, protected login credentials, and necessary security records. Learning content: decks, cards, answers, favorites, review history, study logs, preferences and sharing settings. Support: email, screenshots and issue descriptions you voluntarily submit.

3. Purposes

We process data to manage accounts, authenticate users, show and sync learning content, calculate review progress, keep services secure and respond to support. We do not sell your learning content or use it for cross-app targeted advertising in this version.

4. Local storage and cloud sync

Guest learning records can remain on the device. When you sign in, selected content and sync state may travel over network connections to our servers to enable account-based synchronization. Offline use, sync failures and account changes can affect availability. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete cloud data.

5. System permissions and sharing

We use iOS photo-saving, file-selection or sharing capabilities when you request those actions. External recipients process items under their own policies. Basic card browsing does not require contacts or precise location.

6. Providers and security

Networking, hosting and operating-system services may process technical information necessary to provide the service. We use reasonable access controls, transport security and restricted permissions. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains according to device settings; cloud data is retained while your account and content remain. Necessary security logs may be retained for reasonable periods or legal obligations. Delete cards or decks in-app. For account and associated cloud-data deletion, use Account & Security or request deletion through john@smartnote.ai after identity verification. Export anything important first.

8. Children

Minors should use the app with guardian guidance. Contact support if you believe information about a minor needs review.

9. Your rights and international access

You may request access, correction or deletion and manage permissions through iOS Settings. Infrastructure in other regions may process information for cross-border access subject to applicable law.

10. Updates

We will update this policy as the service changes and provide reasonable notice of material changes. Effective and last updated: October 27, 2026.
