闪念卡片隐私条款支持

技术支持

闪念卡片的使用帮助、故障反馈与账号数据请求。

如何使用卡片

打开首页浏览卡片，轻触进入卡片详情；在卡组里按主题选择内容，练习后可以查看复习计划与学习记录。

登录与云同步

游客内容通常在当前设备保存；登录后可同步账号关联的数据。网络故障会导致登录或同步延迟。出现同步冲突时应确认保留的版本，避免误覆盖。

换机或备份

重要卡片请先使用导出、保存或分享功能留存。游客数据可能随卸载或清理应用数据而丢失。

账号与数据删除

进入「我的 → 设置 → 账号与安全」查看账户管理功能。如当前版本尚无直接删除操作，请发送主题为「闪念卡片账号删除」的邮件至 john@smartnote.ai，提供注册账号标识和可联系的方式。我们将核验归属并告知处理结果。请勿发送密码或完整身份证件。

常见故障

若遇到卡片跳转、学习数据、分享保存或空白页问题，请描述复现步骤，附设备型号、iOS 和 App 版本。发送截图前请遮盖私密内容。

联系渠道

支持邮箱：john@smartnote.ai。也可阅读 隐私政策 与 服务条款。

Technical Support

Help with flashcards, syncing, issue reports and personal-data requests.

Using cards

Browse cards on Home and tap one to see details. Choose decks to practice a topic, then review learning history and due cards.

Login and cloud sync

Guest records generally stay on-device. Signed-in records may sync with your account. Network issues can delay login or sync. When conflicts appear, check which version to preserve.

Changing devices

Export, save or share important cards before changing devices. Guest data may be lost when the app is removed or its local data cleared.

Account and data deletion

Open Profile → Settings → Account & Security to review account management. If a delete option is not available in your version, email john@smartnote.ai with the subject “Flash Thoughts Cards account deletion”, your account identifier and a contact method. We will verify ownership and reply with the result. Never provide passwords or unredacted identity documents.

Troubleshooting

For navigation, sync, export or blank-screen problems, provide reproduction steps, device model, iOS version and app version. Remove sensitive details from screenshots.

Contact

Support email: john@smartnote.ai. See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
