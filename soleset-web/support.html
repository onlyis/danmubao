常见问题FAQ

Q1 鉴别流程是怎样的？Q1 How does authentication work?

买家付款后，卖家将商品寄往平台鉴别中心；鉴别师按六项查验（外观、工艺走线、钢印字迹、材质气味、配件包装、防伪溯源）出具结论与证书，通过后平台直发买家。

After payment, the seller ships to our authentication center. Inspectors run six checks (appearance, stitching, stamps, materials, accessories, anti-counterfeit) and issue a certificate before we ship to the buyer.

Q2 鉴别不通过怎么办？Q2 What if authentication fails?

订单全额退款给买家（含已付费用），商品退回卖家；退款原路返回钱包余额。

The buyer receives a full refund to wallet balance and the item is returned to the seller.

Q3 求购如何成交？Q3 How do bids match?

出价在有效期内有效；当你的出价不低于某尺码最低在售价时，系统提示可直接购买，否则进入求购队列等待卖家接单。

Bids live for their duration. If your bid reaches a size's lowest ask you can buy instantly; otherwise it waits in the queue for a matching listing.

Q4 提现多久到账？Q4 Withdrawal timing?

提现申请经审核后发放，一般 1-3 个工作日；可在钱包收支明细查看进度。

Withdrawals are reviewed then paid, usually within 1–3 business days; track progress in Wallet → Transactions.

Q5 笔记为什么没显示？Q5 Where is my post?

社区笔记先审后发，审核通过后出现在推荐流与话题页；未通过会给出原因。

Posts publish after review; rejected posts include a reason.
