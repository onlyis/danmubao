二、信息的存储与保护2. Storage & Security

  • 数据存储于受访问控制的数据库；密码经 BCrypt 散列，不以明文保存。
  • 传输采用 HTTPS/TLS 加密；接口访问使用令牌鉴权。
  • 鉴别照片与社区图片保存在对象存储/受限目录中，仅授权流程可访问。
  • Data is stored in access-controlled databases; passwords are BCrypt-hashed.
  • Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS; APIs use bearer-token auth.
  • Uploaded photos are stored in restricted storage accessible only to authorized flows.

三、信息的共享3. Sharing

  • 物流环节向承运方（顺丰等）提供必要的收件人与地址信息以完成寄送。
  • 在法律法规要求或司法程序需要时，依法向有权机关提供。
  • 除上述情形外，未经你的同意不向任何第三方共享。
  • Carrier partners (e.g., SF Express) receive recipient details solely to deliver parcels.
  • We disclose information to authorities only where required by law.
  • No other sharing without your consent.

四、保留期限4. Retention

账户与交易数据在服务存续期间保留；鉴别照片自出证之日起保留不超过 12 个月；提现与资金记录按法律要求期限留存。注销账号后，我们将在合理期限内删除或匿名化处理相关数据（法律另有要求的除外）。

Account and transaction data is kept while the service is active; authentication photos are retained for up to 12 months after a certificate is issued; financial records are kept as long as law requires. After account deletion, data is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period unless retention is legally mandated.

五、你的权利5. Your Rights

  • 查询、更正、删除个人信息；导出你的笔记与订单数据。
  • 撤回授权、注销账号（设置内操作或邮件联系）。
  • Access, correct, delete your data; export posts and orders.
  • Withdraw consent or delete your account at any time via settings or email.

六、未成年人6. Children

The service is intended for users aged 14+. If we learn a minor uses it without guardian consent, we will delete their information.
