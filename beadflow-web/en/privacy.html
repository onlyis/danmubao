Privacy Policy

Privacy should be as clear as the app itself: local first, access only when needed, and sharing only when you choose it.

This policy describes the current public version. If cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or another network feature is enabled later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that feature is used.
ScopeDataSharingRetentionSecurityChildrenChanges

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the BeadFlow iOS/iPadOS app and this website. The developer receives privacy requests through the support page.

2. Data the app processes

Projects and canvas content

Pattern grids, layers, colors, titles, tags, and material counts are stored in the app’s local container so projects can be opened, edited, exported, and restored. The current version does not automatically upload this content to a developer server.

Photos and images

The app reads an image only when you actively select it to generate a pattern. Scaling, cropping, quantization, background removal, and color mapping run locally on the device. The app does not scan your entire photo library or send the selected image to the developer.

Settings and preferences

Language, appearance, default canvas dimensions, bead size, brand, grid display, and onboarding state are stored in system preferences on the device to preserve your choices.

Exports and sharing

PNG, PDF, CSV, and project files are created locally first. A file is provided to another app or cloud service only after you use the system share sheet and select that destination. Its later processing is governed by the chosen service.

Diagnostics

The current app includes no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If you enable sharing diagnostics with developers in system settings, Apple may provide aggregate or diagnostic information under Apple’s terms.

3. Device permissions

Photos: used to select an input image. You can change access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → BeadFlow. The current version does not request location, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, or tracking permission.

4. Sale, tracking, and sharing

We do not sell personal data, track you across apps or websites, or build profiles for targeted advertising. The current version has no ad SDK, data broker, or developer-operated account server. Content you deliberately share or export goes only to the destination you choose.

5. Retention, deletion, and migration

Local projects remain until you delete them in the app, uninstall the app, or erase device data. Whether an older copy remains in a system backup depends on your Apple backup settings. See Privacy Choices for deletion and permission guidance. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local container.

6. Security

The app relies on the iOS sandbox, device passcode protections, system file protection, and Apple platform security to isolate local content. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep exports you control for important patterns and protect your device and Apple ID.

7. Children’s privacy

BeadFlow is a general creative tool and is not designed to collect children’s personal information. The current version has no account requirement, public chat, or public profile. Guardians should help children avoid placing sensitive information in shareable QR codes, text patterns, or project names.

8. Your choices and requests

Because current data is primarily on your device, you can directly view, edit, export, or delete projects. For questions, issue reports, or requests available under applicable law, use the support page. Avoid sending sensitive images or full projects unless they are necessary to diagnose the issue.

9. International website access

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting and network providers may process ordinary web logs under their own policies when you visit. Local app projects are not uploaded merely because you open this website.

10. Policy changes

When features, legal requirements, or data practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Where appropriate, notice will also be provided in release notes or in the app.
