From image to physical craft

BeadFlow creates patterns from photos, text, QR codes, templates, and blank canvases. The editor includes real layers, brushes, shapes, selections, mirroring, palette tools, and large canvases. Material counts and row, color, or board assembly modes help turn the design into a finished piece.

Local first

The current version does not require an account. Projects and preferences are stored on the device by default, and photo conversion runs locally. The app currently includes no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud account service.
