YOUR CONTROLS

Privacy Choices

View, export, or delete local projects, manage photo access, and understand what happens after sharing.

Delete one projectOpen its menu in the library and choose Delete. Export a project file first when you need a backup.
Delete all local dataExport anything you need, then use Settings → Storage Management in the app to delete all local data. System backups are controlled by your Apple settings.
Photo accessOpen iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → BeadFlow and change or disable access.
Language and appearanceChoose System, Simplified Chinese, or English and select system, light, or dark appearance in app settings.

Export your content

Export PNG, PDF, CSV, or project files from the editor or project details. You choose the destination. Once delivered to another app, that app’s privacy policy applies.

Revoke a share

BeadFlow cannot recall files you already sent to another app, social service, cloud drive, or recipient. Manage or delete those copies in the destination service.

No advertising tracking

The current version does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or perform cross-app advertising tracking, so there is no in-app ad-tracking switch to disable.

Make a request

The app currently has no developer account database. Most data choices can be completed directly on your device. For other privacy questions, use the Support Center.
