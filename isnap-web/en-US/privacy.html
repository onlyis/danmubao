Privacy Policy

For iSnap on macOS. Last updated: September 21, 2026.

Summary

iSnap is local-first. It does not require an account, does not include advertising tracking, and does not operate an iSnap cloud service that uploads your captures, OCR results, or recordings. Captures, history, pins, OCR results, and recordings are stored on your Mac by default.

1. Data processed locally

  • Captures and images: pixels you capture, import, paste, or edit, plus annotation and crop operations.
  • Capture history: capture time, source app/window title, dimensions, favorites, tags, OCR text, detected codes, and local file references.
  • Pins: image data, position, size, opacity, rotation, flips, groups, and restore state.
  • Recordings: video or GIF content created when you start recording, optionally including system audio, microphone, cursor, or click indicators.
  • Settings: shortcuts, save locations, appearance, OCR, history, pin, and recording preferences.

2. Permissions

  • Screen & System Audio Recording: used for capture, window capture, scrolling capture, and screen recording that you initiate.
  • Accessibility: used only for features such as UI-element snapping, element discovery, and automatic scrolling. iSnap is not designed to log typed content.
  • Microphone: accessed only when you explicitly enable microphone recording.
  • Files: macOS system pickers are used when you choose files or export content.

3. OCR and code recognition

Text, QR-code, and barcode recognition use local macOS technologies such as Apple Vision. The current version does not need to upload recognition images to an iSnap-operated server.

4. Network, accounts, and advertising

The current version does not require an iSnap account, contains no third-party advertising SDK, performs no cross-app advertising tracking, and has no iSnap-operated capture-sync backend. Web links open only when you choose them.

5. Retention, export, and deletion

You can delete capture history and pins in the app. Files you export, share, or copy to another app must be managed at their destination. Removing iSnap deletes data in its app container according to macOS behavior, but does not delete files you exported elsewhere.

6. Apple and third-party services

System capture, Vision, file pickers, and the App Store are governed by Apple's terms and policies. Email or GitHub support submissions are handled by those services. Remove sensitive material before posting publicly.

7. Sensitive content

A screenshot utility may encounter sensitive information visible on screen. Review captures before saving, sharing, or submitting support material, and redact passwords, codes, financial, health, identity, or confidential business information.

8. Contact

For privacy, security, or data questions, visit Technical Support.
