Common questions

Why does iSnap need Screen & System Audio Recording permission?

macOS requires system authorization for apps that capture screen content. If the permission status does not refresh immediately, quit and reopen iSnap.

How does scrolling capture work?

The Mac App Store version uses manual scrolling capture. Choose a target window, scroll its content, and iSnap collects frames for stitching when you finish.

Does OCR upload my image?

The current version uses Apple Vision locally and does not operate an iSnap cloud service for OCR image uploads.

Why is microphone audio missing from a recording?

Enable microphone recording in iSnap and confirm microphone access for iSnap in macOS Privacy & Security settings.

What should I include with a bug report?

  • Mac model and macOS version
  • iSnap version and build
  • The affected feature and exact reproduction steps
  • Expected and actual behavior
  • A redacted screenshot or recording if necessary
