Turn your phone into a glowing LED banner.

Danmu Bao turns your phone into a handheld scrolling LED display — for concerts, airport pickups, birthdays, confessions and cheering. Type any text and play it full-screen as a bright marquee that's easy to read from across the room.

FEATURES
- 100+ text animations: blink, breathe, rainbow, neon, beat and more
- Full customization: font, color, glow, size, scroll speed, direction, background
- Template library: ready-made phrases for concerts, pickups, birthdays and more
- My Banners: save your favorites and reuse them anytime
- Hold up and play: one tap for full-screen playback with auto screen-on and max brightness
- Fully on-device: no sign-up, works offline, collects no personal data

Whether you're at a live show or surprising a friend, Danmu Bao makes your message shine.
